
# 🧭 Kemudi — Complete Brand Kit

## 1. Brand Strategy

### Brand Positioning Statement

*For Malaysians navigating the mental health landscape, Kemudi is the trusted comparison and guidance platform that makes finding the right therapist or service feel clear, affordable, and human — unlike fragmented directories or cold clinical listings, we walk beside you like a knowledgeable friend who helps you make confident choices.*

### Mission

To make mental health support in Malaysia accessible, understandable, and navigable for everyone — regardless of background, budget, or experience.

### Vision

A Malaysia where no one gives up on finding support simply because the path felt too confusing, too expensive, or too overwhelming to walk alone.

### Core Values

| Value | What It Means at Kemudi |
|-------|------------------------|
| **Clarity** | We turn complexity into understanding. No jargon, no confusion. |
| **Warmth** | We lead with humanity. Every interaction feels like a caring conversation. |
| **Respect** | We honour every person's pace, culture, beliefs, and choices. |
| **Transparency** | We show real pricing, real reviews, real differences — no hidden agendas. |
| **Empowerment** | We don't decide for you. We equip you to decide for yourself. |
| **Inclusivity** | We serve all Malaysians — across languages, faiths, and financial realities. |

### Brand Personality Definition

Kemudi is **the warm older sibling who's already been through it.** They don't lecture. They don't diagnose. They sit beside you, listen, and say: *"Here's what I know — let me help you figure out what feels right for you."*

**Personality traits:**

- Calm, never rushed
- Knowledgeable, never condescending
- Warm, never overly familiar
- Clear, never oversimplified
- Grounded, never preachy
- Hopeful, never dismissive of difficulty

**Typography principles:**

- Large, breathable text — never cramped
- Generous white space between sections
- Headings should feel like a calm voice, not a shout
- Body text at minimum 16px for accessibility

### Visual Style Guide

**Shapes & Elements:**

- Rounded corners everywhere (12–16px radius on cards)
- Soft, organic blob shapes as background accents
- No harsh lines or rigid grids — allow breathing room
- Subtle gradients (Mist Sage → Warm Sand) for depth

**Imagery Style:**

- Illustrations preferred over photography (avoids representation issues)
- If photography: real Malaysian people, diverse ethnicities, natural settings, soft lighting
- Illustration style: minimal line art with soft colour fills, warm and inclusive
- Never show people in distress — show people in moments of calm, reflection, or gentle connection

**Spacing & Layout:**

- Apple-inspired generous whitespace
- Content-first hierarchy — let the information breathe
- Cards and containers with soft shadows, not borders
- Maximum content width: ~720px for readability

## 3. Tone of Voice

### Writing Style Guidelines

**Kemudi sounds like:** A warm, knowledgeable friend explaining something clearly over teh tarik.

| Dimension | Kemudi Is | Kemudi Is Not |
|-----------|-----------|---------------|
| Tone | Warm, calm, reassuring | Cold, clinical, robotic |
| Language | Simple, clear, everyday | Jargon-heavy, academic, overly formal |
| Perspective | Beside you (companion) | Above you (expert/authority) |
| Pace | Unhurried, gentle | Urgent, pressuring |
| Cultural voice | Malaysian, grounded | Overly Western, imported |

**Voice principles:**

- Write like you're talking to a friend — not a patient
- Use "you" generously — make it personal
- Short sentences. Clear structure. One idea at a time.
- Acknowledge difficulty without dramatising it
- Always leave the reader feeling more capable, not less

### Example Messaging

**Homepage hero:**

> "Finding the right support shouldn't feel like another thing to figure out alone. We're here to make it clearer."

**Comparison page:**

> "Every therapist works differently. Here's a simple way to see what might suit you."

**Pricing transparency:**

> "No surprises. See real session costs upfront — so you can plan with confidence."

**For someone hesitant:**

> "Not sure where to start? That's completely okay. Let's take it one step at a time."

**Error/empty state:**

> "We couldn't find an exact match — but here are some close options worth exploring."

### Do's and Don'ts in Communication

**✅ Do:**

- Use "you" and "your" — make it personal
- Acknowledge that this is hard ("We know this can feel overwhelming")
- Offer choices, not prescriptions
- Use Malaysian English naturally (it's okay to be local)
- Include Malay where it adds warmth or clarity
- End on a hopeful or empowering note

**❌ Don't:**

- Use diagnosis names or clinical labels
- Say "fix," "cure," "overcome," "disorder," "broken"
- Use "struggles" as a noun or deficit framing
- Pressure urgency ("Don't wait!" "Act now!")
- Assume everyone's context (religion, family situation, financial status)
- Use overly Western idioms or references
- Patronise ("It's easy!" "Just do it!")

## 4. Brand Applications

### Website Homepage — Tone & Layout Direction

**Structure:**

1. **Hero** — Calm, clear headline + single CTA. Warm Sand or Soft Ivory background. Minimal illustration.
2. **How It Works** — 3 simple steps with icons. No overwhelm.
3. **Browse by Need** — Cards (not a wall of text). Let people self-select.
4. **Trust Signals** — Number of providers listed, user testimonials (anonymous-friendly), transparency promise.
5. **Footer** — Resources, languages, accessibility info.

**Feel:** Like walking into a calm, well-lit room where someone says, "Take your time. I'm here when you're ready."

### Social Media Style

- **Visual:** Soft colour palette, illustration-forward, generous whitespace even in posts
- **Content pillars:** Myth-busting, "Did you know?" comparisons, gentle encouragement, provider spotlights, Malaysian mental health literacy
- **Tone:** Conversational, never preachy. Like a friend sharing something helpful.
- **Format:** Carousel posts for education, single-image quotes for encouragement, short reels for "how to use Kemudi"
- **Never:** Before/after framing, trauma content, unsolicited advice

### Pitch Deck Style

- Clean, minimal slides — one idea per slide
- Deep Teal + Warm Sand palette dominates
- Large typography, generous spacing
- Data visualised simply (no complex charts)
- Human stories alongside numbers
- Final slide: clear CTA with warmth

### Email Communication Style

- **Subject lines:** Clear, warm, never clickbait ("Your shortlist is ready" not "DON'T MISS THIS")
- **Opening:** Personal, acknowledging ("Hi [Name], we found a few options that might suit you")
- **Body:** Scannable, short paragraphs, clear next steps
- **Closing:** Empowering ("Take your time exploring — we're here if you need us")
- **Design:** Minimal, warm background, single-column, large text

## Summary

Kemudi's brand is built on a single principle: **clarity is care.** In a space where people feel lost, overwhelmed, or ashamed, the most radical thing you can do is make things simple, warm, and transparent. Every colour, word, and interaction should feel like someone quietly saying: *"You're not alone in figuring this out. Let me help."*
